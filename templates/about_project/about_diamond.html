About Diamond Price Predictor

This project is based on a diamond dataset, where each record represents a diamond with attributes such as weight (carat), cut, color, clarity, and other physical characteristics. To prepare the data for analysis and machine learning, categorical features were encoded numerically.

Encoding Schemes

  • Cut: {'Fair': 0, 'Good': 1, 'Very Good': 2, 'Premium': 3, 'Ideal': 4}
  • Clarity: {'I1': 0, 'SI2': 1, 'SI1': 2, 'VS2': 3, 'VS1': 4, 'VVS2': 5, 'VVS1': 6, 'IF': 7}
  • Color: {'D': 0, 'E': 1, 'F': 2, 'G': 3, 'H': 4, 'I': 5, 'J': 6}

The carat column represents the weight of a diamond in metric carats. A carat is a standard unit of mass equal to 0.2 grams. Therefore:

carat = 0.23 means 0.23 carats (not 23 carats), which equals 0.046 grams.

Clarity Grades Explained

The clarity of a diamond refers to the visibility of internal flaws (inclusions) or surface imperfections (blemishes). Below is a detailed breakdown:

Grade Stands For Flaw Visibility Value Level Encoding Scheme
I1 Included 1 Visible to eye Low 0
SI2 Slightly Included 2 Possibly visible Low-Mid 1
SI1 Slightly Included 1 Hard to see Mid 2
VS2 Very Slightly Included 2 Difficult at 10× magnification Mid-High 3
VS1 Very Slightly Included 1 Very difficult at 10× High 4
VVS2 Very Very Slightly Included 2 Extremely hard at 10× Very High 5
VVS1 Very Very Slightly Included 1 Almost invisible Very High 6
IF Internally Flawless No internal flaws Premium 7

Color Grades Explained

The color of a diamond refers to the presence of any color in the diamond, with the most valuable diamonds being those with the least amount of color. The grading scale starts from "D" (colorless) to "J" (light yellow or brown). Below is a breakdown:

Grade Color Description Value Level Encoding Scheme
D Colorless Premium 0
E Colorless Very High 1
F Colorless High 2
G Near Colorless Mid-High 3
H Near Colorless Mid 4
I Near Colorless Low-Mid 5
J Light Yellow/Brown Low 6

Cut Grades Explained

The cut of a diamond refers to how well the diamond's facets are shaped and aligned to reflect light. A well-cut diamond will sparkle more and have better overall appearance and value. Below is a breakdown of the cut grades:

Grade Cut Description Value Level Encoding Scheme
Fair Poorly cut diamond, minimal sparkle Low 0
Good Acceptable cut with moderate sparkle Low-Mid 1
Very Good Well cut diamond with noticeable sparkle Mid 2
Premium Excellent cut with strong sparkle Mid-High 3
Ideal Perfectly cut diamond, maximized sparkle High 4

These preprocessing and explanation steps help standardize the dataset for analysis and model development while preserving the meaningful ranking of each feature.
